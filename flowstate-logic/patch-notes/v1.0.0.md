# v1.0.0 - Production launch

The first full production release. FlowState Logic is out of prelaunch,
and this is the MVP going out to everyone.

## 🚀 New

- **Ember economy rebuilt from the ground up.** Embers now cap at
  **5** and refill **1 every 2 hours** - the old reserve-ember pool
  is gone entirely, replaced by a simpler, clearer system.
- **Pro's headline perk is unlimited attempts.** An active Pro
  subscription never loses an ember on a wrong answer, anywhere in
  the app - no ember gate at all.
- **Free accounts now earn a small weekly Cores trickle** in
  addition to existing Capstone/Chapter/curriculum rewards.
- **Cosmetics show real rarity.** Shop and Wardrobe tiles now
  display Common, Uncommon, Epic, Legendary, and Artifact tiers with
  rarity-colored borders.
- **Founders Pack** - a limited-run Shop item (Founder's Embercoil
  skin, $9.99, one-time) for early supporters. Available for a
  90-day window from launch.
- **Company splash screen.** The app now opens on the Cinderway
  wordmark before the title-screen splash plays.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Chapter and Advanced Practice progress bars now fill in the
  right direction and show real partial progress,** instead of
  jumping to "100% full" after just the first lesson.
- **Android's splash screen no longer crops the wordmark.**
- Settings now correctly marks JavaScript/TypeScript "Coming Soon"
  under Primary Language.
- Pro / Yearly copy no longer promises a specific future price
  increase - reworded to "Locked in for early subscribers."
- Lifetime is no longer offered on the in-app Pro screen
  (Monthly/Yearly only).
